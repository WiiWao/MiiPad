# MiiPad

PIC HERE

The MiiPad is a 12 key custom keypad made with mechanical switches, a magnetic case, and a microcontroller. It's fully programmable, supports multi-layer, tap/hold control, and even full on macros.

## Parts

- 1x 3D Printed Case
- 1x Custom PCB
- 1x Microcontroller Board (Xiao RP2040)
- 12x Diodes (SOD-123)
- 12x MX Style Mechanical Switches (Cherry MX Browns)
- 12x MX Style Keycaps (Extras, to your preference is fine)

## Tools and Technology

- 3D printer
- Soldering Equipment
- KiCad
- Blender
- QMK

## Design

The keypad was designed with space and functionality in mind. The small and minimal design paired with the small Xiao RP2040 makes a good combinations for space efficiency. QMK firmware supports easy reprogrammability and some of the more advanced functions like macros.

- PCB made in KiCad, manufactured by JLCPCB
- Case modeled in blender, 3D printed myself (Alternative: JLC3DP)
- Microcontroller, switches, and diodes all hand-soldered
- Firmware built with QMK, including custom keymaps and macros
